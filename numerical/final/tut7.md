Polynomial:
$$
a_{0} + a_{1}x
$$
Interpolate the points:
$$
a_{0}=1 \qquad a_{0}+a_{1}=3 \qquad a_{0}+3a_{1}=13
$$
First and second equations say,
$$
a_{0}+a_{1}=3\implies a_{1}=3-1=2
$$
Third equation says,
$$
a_{0}+3a_{1} = 1+3(2) = 7=13
$$
Which is false and therefore no polynomial of degree 1 interpolates these three points
