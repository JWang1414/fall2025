### Current and EMF in Imperfect Conductors
Inside real conductors, electronic motion is impeded by scattering processes.

Imagine a series of electrons travelling through a material. We expect these electrons, travelling at $v_{f}\approx 10^{8}$ cm/s will make frequent collisions. Generally speaking, when there is no applied field, there is also no net current in the material.
