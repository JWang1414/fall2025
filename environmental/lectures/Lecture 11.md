# Understanding the Economy
The modern basis of the market economy is based on the study of scarce resources, and human desire. It is based on increasing growth and consumption, building competition, and establishing private property.

The idea of the gift economy is one where excess is given to share. Abundance is given away, as opposed to hoarded (to build artificial scarcity). Nurtures the community and creates a society where the economic unit is more than the individual person.
- Builds gratitude as a sort of currency
# The Paris Economy
The work done in this field has already changed the future. 10 years ago, it was expected that the world would be 4-5 C hotter by the end of the century, but that has changed to be roughly 2.8 C.

We can still go further, though. If all the countries' Nationally Determined Contributions are implemented, warming is estimated to be ~2.4 C.
# Anthro-harmonic
The view of the world that places humans within the world and the ecosystem, as opposed to conquerors of the ecosystem.

The ultimate goal established by numerous people within this course is to curb our growth and insatiability to move towards a sustainable civilisation.

To learn to love the Earth and Gaia is one way we can help to establish this movement. This change begins to love because we need to find a real reason and motivation to change, to improve.
- If you accept Gaia, than you cannot hurt the Earth, because you are also hurting yourself
- The same applied to the land ethic, gratitude, and reverence for life
# Beavers
Social animals with a strong family structure. Their diligent construction of dams increases biodiversity, improve water quality, and minimise floods. We should be more like them.
# Abuelas de Plaza de Mayo
During an Argentinian dictatorship, young people were abducted and persecuted. In response, their parents began to petition in the Plaza de Mayo to regain their children.

This is the idea of hope encouraged within this course. Hope should not be something that is baseless. One must push through danger and impossible odds in an attempt to legitimately move forward. Be brave, but be directed.
# Think like a Mountain
Develop a love, respect, and admiration for the land, and understand that you are just a member of the biotic community. Expand your mindset to include a view point that is not anthropocentric. Familiarise yourself with trophic cascades, everything in nature is connected, it is hubris to think humans are in control.
