### Food Processing
Generally speaking, companies will try to obfuscate the production strategies of food. They will present classical images of farming, while in the background raise farm animals in abusive factory settings, while abusing the workers who prepare the food.

As we have already discussed, the chickens have been modified in such a way where they grow so quickly that their bones cannot keep up. They cannot walk, they cannot raise themselves, they are pumped with antibiotics, and they live and die without having moved from the same small area.

Oftentimes hired workers are undocumented immigrants, people who legally have no real rights, and so corporations abuse these workers, pay them very little, etc. Because these people have little other choice, they are all but forced to take the jobs.

### Climate Change Debt
Historically, the wealthiest countries have emitted the most greenhouse gases. Countries like the UK, Germany, and the US are at the top. India and China are lower down just because they started developing (industrially) later.

Contrary to this, countries that have historically emitted less, like northern South America, Africa, the middle East and Indonesia are far more at risk from climate change. So there is a dynamic where the ones that have emitted the most are the least vulnerable.

### Environmental Racism
There are strong correlations between waste facility sites and demographic characteristics. That is, dangerous facilities are more likely to be built near or within the communities of minority groups.

Mossville, Louisiana, a predominantly African American community was subject to a company dumping potential human carcinogens into their water supply.

There are just a few examples.
