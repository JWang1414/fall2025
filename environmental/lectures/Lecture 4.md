### The Chain of Courage
The idea that one courageous person can inspire a number to follow. After Carson, for example, chemical scientists secretly sent her research papers revealing the knowledge the industry had about DDT.

Carson's publishers, again, refused to stop publishing her books despite threats from chemical companies.

Interviewers and broadcast networks refused to stop airing footage of her discussions.

Much of the movement in the environmental history, like this, are possible only because of this concept.
### France
In World War II, we found that once France fell, people realized the strength of the Nazis, and so began to mobilize. People were forced to move and take action. Companies were told by the government to redirect their effort.

Often times, because private companies were looking for a profit, they might overcharge for their war products, but they were competing the government funded projects, ones that weren't product focused. If they wanted to make a profit at all, their prices would have to compare with the honest prices.

These events tell us that, given enough force, humans can mobilise and push forward significant change. Applying this to the climate crisis, we can perhaps extrapolate and claim we can accomplish the same thing here. Though, one must has: "What is our 'France' that must fall?"
