### The science of Gaia theory
Natural systems regulate:
- $CO_{2}$ in the atmosphere
- The temperature and sulphur quantities
- Salinity of the oceans
### Compatibility with evolution
There was strong opposition from the Darwinism community. This was pushed by the thinking that, within the ecosystem, "nothing is given for free". That is, there is no reason for creatures to help regular the environment to help other lifeforms.

However, it has now been shown that Gaia theory is within the evolutionary parameters of Darwinism.

Evolution is not all competition, cooperation exists within it as well, and it is just as important. A fundamental example is serial endosymbiosis, the concept that mitochondrion and chloroplasts were originally external animals. Symbiosis, of course, exists in numerous other systems in the ecosystem.
### Power of myth and metaphor
Metaphors help to communicate scientific ideas in a way that is easier to grasp and understand.

In particular, it was so powerful with Gaia because it massively increased the amount of public support for the theory. This means that not only does Gaia work as an interdisciplinary scientific theory, but also as a part of the environmental movement.

The acceptance of Gaia not just as a theory but reality implies that you yourself accept that you are part of Gaia. It seeds a sense of interconnected-ness in the human mind, that we have a part to play in environmental balance.
